Hi, I'm Rajnish Kumar — a Red Team Professional.

Myself Rajnish Kumar, a cybersecurity professional with a deep passion for the offensive side of security. Since graduating with my B.Tech in Computer Science, I've spent more than 4 years in the industry, focusing on Red Teaming and penetration testing (VAPT).
